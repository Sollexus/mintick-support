Common questions

How do I track work and breaks?

Set your hourly rate in Settings or tap the rate on Timer. Start earning begins a session. Take a break pauses paid time. Resume continues tracking at your current rate. Finish session lets you add a note and save it to history.

I forgot to start the timer. Can I add time?

Open Reports and tap Log time. Choose the start and end times, hourly rate, currency, and optional note. Log separate entries around unpaid breaks. Mintick rejects overlapping paid time and future entries.

What appears on the Lock Screen?

On supported iOS versions, an active session can show a Live Activity with live elapsed paid time and pause, resume, refresh, and finish controls. The earnings amount is a timestamped snapshot. Tap refresh for an updated amount. Allow Live Activities for Mintick if iOS asks. iOS can dismiss or limit the lifetime of activities; the session continues independently.

Which time zone do reports use?

Reports and manual time entry use your device’s local time zone. Sessions are grouped under the day they started, including sessions that cross midnight. Reports include saved sessions; the Timer screen also shows your active earnings.

Can I edit or delete a saved session?

Open Reports, tap the session, and review its paid segments, rates, currency, and note. You can edit it or use Delete session. Deletion is permanent in the app. Discard session removes only the current unsaved session.

Where is my history stored?

On your device. Mintick has no account or app-operated cloud sync. Device backups may include app data depending on your Apple settings. Uninstalling Mintick may remove your history. There is currently no in-app CSV export or backup restore feature.

Why are currency amounts estimates?

Mintick uses cached exchange rates that refresh when online. It does not calculate taxes, deductions, overtime rules, or employer payroll. Offline reports can use older or approximate bundled rates.
