Your work stays yours.

This policy explains how the Mintick iPhone and iPad app handles information, and how support requests and this website work.

Information stored on your device

Mintick stores work sessions, session notes, hourly rates, currencies, settings, achievements, and cached exchange rates locally on your device. These records are used to calculate estimated earnings and display your history. The app does not send these work records to the developer.

No accounts, advertising, or tracking

Mintick does not require an account and does not include advertising, advertising identifiers, third-party analytics, or tracking across other apps and websites.

Currency exchange requests

When connected, Mintick periodically requests exchange rates from Frankfurter over HTTPS. It requests USD-based exchange rates; it does not include your pay, hours, notes, session history, name, or email. Network infrastructure necessarily receives connection information such as your IP address while serving a request. Frankfurter states that it does not retain personal data, IP addresses, or request URLs and uses Cloudflare for caching and DDoS protection. Its practices are described on its website.

Lock Screen and device features

Live Activities display session information on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island through iOS. Anyone able to view those surfaces may see it. You can manage Live Activities in iOS settings. Haptics and the optional keep-screen-awake setting operate on your device. Mintick does not use remote push notifications to upload your session data.

Backups and deletion

Apple device backups may include local app data, depending on your device and iCloud settings. Apple manages those backups under its own terms and privacy practices. You can delete saved sessions in Reports. Uninstalling the app may remove its local history. Mintick does not maintain a server copy from which it can restore your work records.

If you contact support

If you email sollexus@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. Email is handled through the email providers involved. Support correspondence is used to respond and resolve your request, and retained as needed for that purpose. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive details. You can email to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

This website

These support pages do not set app-specific analytics or advertising cookies and contain no analytics scripts or contact forms. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement. This website’s hosting is separate from the locally stored data in the Mintick app.

Changes and contact

This policy may change if Mintick’s practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions or requests, contact the Mintick developer at sollexus@gmail.com.
